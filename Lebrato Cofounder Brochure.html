The business

The problem with celebrating in the UK is that finding the right place is way too hard

You work in hospitality. You already know this. How often do you see groups of people come in, hen parties, birthday dinners, leaving dos, who clearly booked the wrong kind of place? They wanted private dining and got a table in the corner. They wanted a vibe and got a chain restaurant. They wanted something memorable and got something mediocre.

The reason this happens is that finding the right venue for a group takes an enormous amount of effort. You're Googling, switching tabs between OpenTable, Yelp, and a dozen restaurant websites, reading 40 reviews for places that turn out to be fully booked anyway. The whole process is broken.

Lebrato fixes this. You tell it what you're celebrating, "birthday dinner for 14, around £60 a head, East London, Saturday evening", and it gives you three perfect answers in seconds. Not a list of hundreds. Three. With an explanation of exactly why each one fits your occasion. Then you book straight through the app.

The vesting schedule works like this. Think of it as your shares being "unlocked" over time, month by month, as long as you're actively contributing to the business.

0

Day 1: Agreement Signed

150,000 shares are formally allocated in your name. You are a co-founder of Lebrato Limited. However, if you leave before Month 12, the company has the right to buy them back at nominal value (essentially nothing). This is the cliff.

12

Month 12: The Cliff (25% Vests Immediately)

At the 12-month anniversary of the agreement, 37,500 shares (25% of your total) fully vest and are yours unconditionally. If you leave after this point, you keep these shares permanently.

13-48

Months 13 - 48: Monthly Vesting

The remaining 112,500 shares vest gradually at approximately 3,100 shares per month over the next 36 months. By month 48 (4 years total), all 150,000 shares are fully and unconditionally yours.

✓

Why Does It Work This Way?

Vesting is industry standard everywhere from Silicon Valley to Shoreditch. It ensures both co-founders stay committed for the long term. Naadhish's shares are subject to the same principle. It's not a penalty, it's a structure that makes the company investable and both of us accountable.
